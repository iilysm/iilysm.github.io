Developing a mixed reality application to support children in understanding everyday data collection patterns

I wrote my bachelor's thesis on developing a Mixed Reality (MR) application designed to educate children aged 10–12 about data collection and privacy on their personal devices.

The primary goal was to create an engaging experience that simplifies the complex topic of data collection, making it appropriate for a child’s understanding of how their phones and tablets truly operate.

Motivation
This work is motivated by the critical need for digital literacy education during a pivotal developmental stage; in Germany, phone ownership jumps significantly for children in the 10-12 age bracket. While traditional instruction exists, this project leverages a gamification approach to actively empower children to become informed subjects regarding their digital footprint.

This thesis builds upon a prior master’s thesis that established the theoretical design and early-stage prototyping, advancing the concept to a fully realized, immersive application.

The application is architected for Apple Vision Pro, utilizing a powerful stack of technologies to blend the digital and physical worlds: SwiftUI, Combine, RealityKit, and ARKit.

Key features of the experience:

  • Guided Exploration: A user is presented with a prepped physical phone and tablet, where a virtual character provides hints for interaction.

  • Data Visualization: Actions like listening to music trigger the drop of virtual information orbs near the physical device.

  • Physical-Digital Anchoring: Users are encouraged to collect these orbs and add them to a "portal" anchored to a real-world object (a moon-like lamp), using physical space as the primary interactive canvas.

  • Inferred Data:The character explains how personal profiles are built from collected data, and an inventory-style overview. After collection, the data is processed and visualized, demonstrating how inferred data (assumptions about the user based on collected data) is generated and added to the profile.

Findings
We anticipated that this highly immersive experience will lead to a significantly increased awareness of data collection and inferential profiling. Our results confirm the potential of this application.

Technical Details
  • Hardware Platform: Apple Vision Pro (visionOS)
  • Tech Stack: SwiftUI, Combine, RealityKit, ARKit
  • Paradigm: Mixed Reality (MR) & Tangible User Interfaces
  • Target Audience: Children (10–12 years)
